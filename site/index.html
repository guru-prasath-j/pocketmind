Features

A study assistant that lives in your pocket

Everything you need to learn from your own documents, with nothing sent to anyone else.

Local RAG search

Documents are chunked and embedded into an on-device vector store, so answers come from the most relevant passages.

On-device LLM

A quantized Gemma model runs through MediaPipe on your phone's GPU and streams answers word by word.

Private by design

No accounts, no telemetry, no API keys. Your notes stay on your device, even in airplane mode.

Answers with sources

Formatted answers with headings, lists and code, plus the exact passages each answer was grounded in.

Organized library

Search, sort, star and revisit recent documents. Every document keeps its own conversation.

Light & dark themes

A premium violet design in both themes. Follow your system setting or pick one yourself.

How it works

Retrieval-augmented generation, on your phone

Instead of answering from memory, PocketMind finds the right parts of your document first, then asks the model to answer using only those.

Import

Pick a PDF, text or Markdown file. Text is extracted on-device with syncfusion_flutter_pdf.

Chunk & embed

The text is split into overlapping 600-character chunks and embedded into vectors stored in local SQLite.

Retrieve

Your question is embedded too, and the top 4 most similar chunks are found by cosine similarity.

Generate

Gemma answers from those chunks only and streams the reply, with sources you can expand.

Screens

Designed to feel premium

Rounded cards, soft violet glows and smooth motion. Switch the page theme to preview both modes.

Privacy

Your notes never leave your phone

PocketMind makes zero network calls to answer a question. The model, the vector store and your documents all live in the app's private storage.

  • No sign-up, accounts or API keys
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • Works fully in airplane mode
  • Delete a document and its chunks are gone

Get started

Run it on your Android phone

PocketMind runs natively on Android and iOS. The AI model is bundled with the app, so clone the repo and run it on a connected device.

terminal
$ git clone https://github.com/guru-prasath-j/pocketmind.git
$ cd pocketmind
$ flutter pub get
$ flutter run  # on a connected Android device
